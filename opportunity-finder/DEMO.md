# 30-Second Demo

## Demo prompt

> Scan the last 7 days for new pain created by heavy AI users. Focus on Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw and AI-assisted professional workflows. I want micro-products that can be built in one day, have near-zero seller runtime cost and a natural distribution path. Check real demand and current competition first. It is acceptable to recommend nothing.

## What a good run should visibly do

1. Search multiple workflow domains rather than only coding-agent communities.
2. Benchmark at least one current winner and identify an adjacent unresolved job.
3. Use original sources with dates for material claims.
4. Kill platform incidents, trivial workarounds, mature free incumbents and technically inaccessible ideas.
5. Estimate build hours and identify who pays model/API/runtime cost.
6. Return at most three finalists — or explicitly return `0 SHIP`.
7. Produce a clickable Source Ledger.

## Live-host acceptance example

The final WorkBuddy acceptance scan on 2026-08-26 covered five workflow domains, evaluated ten candidates, completed three traceable Winner Benchmarks, produced traceable evidence for all ten candidates, and still returned `0 SHIP / 1 WATCH / 9 SKIP`. This is expected behavior: Opportunity Finder is allowed to say there is nothing worth shipping today.

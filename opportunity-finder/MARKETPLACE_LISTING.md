## One-line pitch
Find fresh AI-native micro-product opportunities worth shipping now — then kill the weak ones before you waste a weekend building them.

## Short description
A free Agent Skill for solo builders and AI power users. It scans fresh demand, benchmarks winning Skills/plugins/MCP tools, checks live competition, verifies technical feasibility, estimates seller economics, and returns at most three evidence-backed `SHIP / TEST / WATCH / SKIP` decisions.

## Long description
Opportunity Finder is not another generic startup-idea generator. It is optimized for builders who can ship Skills, MCP tools, CLIs, utilities, micro-apps, and other digital products quickly.

It searches recent AI power-user pain, studies what is already winning, finds adjacent unresolved jobs, checks free incumbents and platform-native fixes, verifies whether the proposed product can actually be built, and estimates build time plus who pays the model/API/runtime cost.

A valid result can be `0 SHIP`. The goal is to save build time, not manufacture optimism.


# Anti-Saturation Rules

Opportunity Finder is designed to exploit small windows, not avoid all competition forever.

## Usually skip

- generic AI writing, summaries, SEO, PPT/PDF, image prompts, general scraping;
- broad “AI assistant for X” wrappers without a concrete failure mode;
- categories with a mature free incumbent and no clear narrower job;
- products that need paid ads to reach the first users;
- ideas whose main value is a scoring formula users can trivially recreate with a prompt;
- low-priced products with high expected support burden;
- lifetime-priced products that require seller-paid recurring model/API cost.

## Competition states

- `empty`: no dedicated solution found;
- `fragmented`: workarounds and small tools, no clear winner;
- `forming`: multiple focused products arriving now;
- `crowded`: mature substitutes/strong incumbent.

A `forming` market can still be a SHIP if:

- the window is fresh;
- build time is very small;
- natural distribution is strong;
- seller marginal cost is near zero;
- the wedge is concrete and demoable.

## Do not overvalue defensibility

For this strategy, “could be copied in two weeks” is not a rejection by itself. Compare copy speed with build/launch speed and expected harvest period.
